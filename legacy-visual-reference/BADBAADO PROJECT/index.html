About BADBAADO

A referral network built for speed and safety

BADBAADO is a paperless referral platform for hospitals. Instead of phone calls, guesswork, and handwritten notes, it moves a structured referral packet — patient summary, triage level, department, and a scannable QR badge — from one hospital to another in seconds.

When a referral is critical, the receiving Emergency Room gets a pre-arrival alert before the ambulance arrives, so the right team, bed, and equipment are waiting.

How It Works

From referral to ready, in four steps

Every movement of a patient between hospitals follows the same clear path.

1

Refer

The referring doctor selects a receiving hospital, department, and triage level and submits the referral packet.

2

Alert

The receiving ER receives a pre-arrival alert with the triage level, condition summary, and patient QR badge.

3

Prepare

The receiving team accepts the handoff and prepares the room, staff, and equipment before arrival.

4

Arrive & treat

On arrival, the receiving team scans the QR badge for instant, verified patient and referral details. No lost notes.

Emergency Referral

Every second counts. Alert the ER before the ambulance arrives.

In an emergency, BADBAADO's fast-referral mode sends a critical alert to the receiving hospital in under a minute. The ER sees the triage level instantly and can confirm the bed before the patient reaches the door.

  • One-click emergency referral widget
  • Live triage level shown to the receiving ER
  • Accept & prepare confirmation from the receiving team

Emergency Triage Levels

I

Critical (Red)

Immediate, life-threatening condition. Requires rapid resuscitation — e.g. cardiac arrest, severe trauma.

II

Emergent (Orange)

Potentially life-threatening. Needs treatment within minutes — e.g. stroke, acute chest pain.

III

Urgent (Yellow)

Serious but stable. Should be treated as soon as possible — e.g. severe abdominal pain.

IV

Non-urgent (Green)

Minor condition. Can wait — e.g. minor cuts, routine follow-ups.
